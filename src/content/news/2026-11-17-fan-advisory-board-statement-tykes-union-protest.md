<p>It is with genuine disappointment that the 14 members of Tykes Union have decided to proceed with their planned protest on 20th November.</p>
<p>We met with Tykes Union. Following our initial meeting, we believed the requests made were reasonable and achievable through constructive dialogue with the Club. On that basis, we asked for the protest to be postponed to allow progress to be made.</p>
<p>Sadly, Tykes Union subsequently changed those requests and introduced new timescales which we believed were unrealistic and unreasonable. What began as positive discussions increasingly felt like an ultimatum, with a gun to our heads. We are left feeling that, regardless of what progress could have been achieved, they had already decided that protesting was their preferred outcome.</p>
<p>To clarify, we share the frustrations of Barnsley supporters and fully respect their right to peaceful protest. However, we disagree with this approach and believe constructive engagement offers a better opportunity to deliver meaningful change while protecting our club and supporting the players on the pitch.</p>
<p>The FAB will be present at the Barnsley Supporters Trust meeting on Wednesday 18th November, listening to supporters, understanding their concerns and working together to identify the questions that need answering. Our intention is to take those questions directly to the club and seek the transparency, accountability and progress supporters deserve.</p>
<p>The FAB remains committed to working with all supporters and supporters' groups, including Tykes Union. Our door remains open, regardless of differing opinions.</p>
<p>We all want a better Barnsley Football Club. We must find a way to work together rather than allow our frustrations to divide us.</p>
<p>Barnsley FC Fan Advisory Board</p>
