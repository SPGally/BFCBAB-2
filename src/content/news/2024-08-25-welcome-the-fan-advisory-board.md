<p>Barnsley FC proudly introduces the newly formed Fan Advisory Board (FAB), a vital initiative aimed at strengthening communication between the club and its supporters.</p><p>The FAB is made up of representatives from various supporter groups, including away day travellers, season ticket holders, women and girls, disabled fans, and more. Each board member is dedicated to advocating for their respective communities, ensuring that every fan has a platform to voice their concerns, ideas, and feedback.</p><p>If you have questions, issues, or anything you'd like to raise with the club, we encourage you to select the&nbsp;<strong>‘Submit your ideas’</strong>&nbsp;button on this website and choose the FAB member you’d like to direct your message to.</p><p>Your voice matters—let’s work together to make a difference for Barnsley FC and its supporters!</p>
