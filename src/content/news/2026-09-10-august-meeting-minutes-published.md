<p>The minutes of the Fan Advisory Board meeting held on Tuesday 4 August 2026 at Oakwell have now been published. You can read them on our <a href="/minutes">Minutes page</a> or on the <a href="https://www.barnsleyfc.co.uk/fans/fan-advisory-board/fab-meeting-minutes" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">official club website</a>.</p>
<h2>An apology for the delay</h2>
<p>First, an apology. These minutes have taken longer than usual to reach you. Every set of minutes is checked and signed off by the club before it is published, and this time they were with the club for considerably longer than normal. The people who review them were, quite rightly, focused on the transfer window and the start of the season, and there simply weren't the resources to turn them around at the usual pace. We know supporters want to see what was discussed while it is still fresh, and we will keep working with the club to get future minutes out more quickly.</p>
<h2>What was discussed</h2>
<p>It was a full agenda, the first meeting after the close-season break. The highlights:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>A visit from Daniel Stendel.</strong> The Head Coach joined us for the first half hour, spoke about bringing back passion and belief, asked for patience while the new squad settles, and said he was keen to see more moments like the players going over to the East Stand after the Lincoln friendly.</li>
  <li><strong>The Independent Football Regulator.</strong> The final licensing guidance was published on 1 July. Our own comparison with the earlier draft found little of substance had changed, and the club expects to be asked for a fan engagement plan once the Regulator issues a template.</li>
  <li><strong>Your questions.</strong> Supporters asked about home friendlies and a possible pre-season tour, kit release dates, hotel discounts for travelling fans, car parking and post-match road closures, the new club offices (funded by a ring-fenced council grant that cannot be spent on football) and progress on a fanzone.</li>
  <li><strong>DX Corner Stand top floor.</strong> The most heavily discussed item. Season ticket holders on the top floor of the Corner Stand were moved to make way for a sponsorship arrangement, and those moved down to the Patrick's level were not offered the discount given to those moved up. The club accepted this was a fair criticism, agreed to look at price protection for those supporters, and committed to bringing decisions of this scale to the FAB before they are finalised in future.</li>
  <li><strong>Accessibility.</strong> Options for Auracast audio for visually impaired and hard-of-hearing supporters, clearer signage for the accessible ticket office counter, a smaller disabled supporter passport card, and recording access needs once rather than every visit.</li>
  <li><strong>Community and 2027.</strong> New Community Trust funding for themed matchdays through the season, and a working group forming for the club's 2027 anniversary celebrations. Volunteers are wanted.</li>
  <li><strong>The big screen.</strong> A long-standing request. A larger screen has been costed at roughly £55,000 to £60,000, and the club will check whether staged payments could make it achievable.</li>
</ul>
<p>The minutes also record a plaque being commissioned in memory of Reverend Tiverton Preedy, the club's founder, and confirm that an in-person fans' forum is planned this season.</p>
<h2>Next meeting</h2>
<p>The next meeting is being arranged for mid-September. If there is something you would like us to raise with the club, <a href="/submit">send it to us</a> and we will add it to the fans' questions.</p>
