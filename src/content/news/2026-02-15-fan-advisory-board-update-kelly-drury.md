<p>Kelly Drury has formally informed the board that she has stepped down from the Fan Advisory Board and from her role as Chair.</p><p>As a result, Steve Brain will step up to take over as Chair, and a new Vice-Chair will be voted in by the board in due course.</p><p>The board would like to place on record its sincere thanks to Kelly for the significant time, commitment, and hard work she has put into the Fan Advisory Board at Barnsley Football Club. Her contribution and dedication to supporter representation are greatly appreciated.</p><p>Further communication will follow shortly regarding the application process to appoint a replacement representative for the Away Ticketing and Traveller role.</p>
