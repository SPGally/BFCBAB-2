<p>WE'RE LAUNCHING A PODCAST!</p><p><strong>The Barnsley FC Fan Advisory Board is launching its very own podcast — and our first episode, covering everything discussed at our March 2026 meeting, is coming very soon.</strong></p><p>We've been thinking for a while about how to better get the FAB's message out to supporters. The meetings are valuable — we sit down with people at the very top of the club, including CEO Jon Flatman, and have frank, detailed conversations about what's really going on at Oakwell. But not everyone has the time to read through meeting minutes, and we want as many Reds fans as possible to feel connected to what the FAB is doing on their behalf.</p><p>So we're doing something about it.</p><p>After every FAB meeting, we'll be releasing a podcast episode that walks you through everything that was discussed — the key points, what the club said, what supporters raised, and what it all means for you. No jargon, no spin. Just a straight-talking breakdown you can listen to on the bus, in the car, or wherever you get your football fix.</p><p>Our very first episode covers the March 2026 FAB meeting, and there is plenty to get into — from the club's finances and the wider state of League One football, to season ticket pricing, new supporter benefits, and more. It's dropping very soon, so make sure you're subscribed so you don't miss it.</p><p>🎙 WHERE TO LISTEN</p><p>The Barnsley FC Fan Advisory Board podcast is available on all major podcast platforms.
